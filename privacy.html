Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

This policy describes how Homebound (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our software and services that connect to third-party platforms, including Meta (Facebook) products when you authorize access.

1. Information we process

Depending on your use of the app and optional integrations, we may process:

  • Account and profile identifiers provided by a platform (for example, a Meta user ID) when you sign in or grant permissions.
  • Content you choose to import (for example, posts or media you export or request) for display in your personal archive or site.
  • Technical data such as server logs (IP address, timestamps, HTTP requests) needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide the features you request (e.g. import, display, and publish your content), to maintain security, and to comply with law. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Meta / Facebook platform

If you connect a Meta account, our use of information received from Meta is governed by your Facebook app settings and Meta’s Platform Terms. We only request permissions and data needed for the app’s stated functionality.

4. Retention and deletion

We retain data only as long as needed for the service or as required by law. You may request deletion of data associated with your use of Homebound as described on our User Data Deletion page.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Vladimir Yakunin at vyakunin@gmail.com.

6. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Vladimir Yakunin is the Data Controller of your personal information. If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific data protection rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at vyakunin@gmail.com. We have one month to respond to your request.

You also have the right to complain to a Data Protection Authority about our collection and use of your Personal Data. For more information, please contact the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy where required by law.
