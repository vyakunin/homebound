Homebound — own your social-media archive

Your posts will outlive the platforms. Homebound is a set of open-source tools: browser extensions that export your own Facebook and X history (everything stays on your machine), and a self-hosted blog that imports it, makes it searchable, and lets you talk to it with an LLM.
